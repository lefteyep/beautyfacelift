## Initial scope

Agency-only list, search/status filters, contact actions, notes/history and manually recorded appointment/outcome. Include staff entry of telephone/WhatsApp enquiries and recovery of missed website enquiries: without that, the operator cannot perform the required reconciliation. No booking inference from tracking clicks. No new revenue reports, weekly conversion rate, newsletter, imports, Treatwell parsing or Phase 2. Existing protected revenue code remains for compatibility; revenue fields and misleading weekly KPIs are absent from the initial UI.

The workflow is described in [COCKPIT-PILOT-WORKFLOW.md](COCKPIT-PILOT-WORKFLOW.md). Staff recovery uses the original enquiry UUID and exact ISO received timestamp from the notification email; its event records the source, recovery reason and staff action. Replays/concurrent recovery cannot overwrite an existing record. Manually entered/recovered records send no email and are excluded from automatic reminders/confirmation.

## Safe defaults and failure handling

`LEADS_CAPTURE=off` and `LEADS_REMINDERS=off` are committed defaults. Deployment alone must not activate enquiry persistence or reminders. Customer mail remains unset/off. Access team/AUD stay empty until the chosen login is actually configured; the Worker denies unauthorized access. No Teresa invitation, test email or cockpit link is introduced.

The separately protected [D1 erasure procedure](LEAD-D1-ERASURE.md) is also off by default (`LEADS_DATA_OPERATIONS=off`). Ordinary staff access does not authorize it: it requires an agency identity in `LEADS_DATA_OPERATORS`, same-origin CSRF, exact enquiry IDs, a private case code, a reviewed snapshot digest and typed confirmation. Unknown schema, ambiguous copies and changed data stop the operation. Cleanup, erasure and verification share one guarded batch. After a batch exception the result is unconfirmed, including a possible lost response after commit; the operator must inspect the case before retrying. No real record has been erased.

After an approved capture rollout, every accepted public submission has one system-generated UUID/time across D1, owner notification and any storage-failure log. A D1 error/missing binding still permits the existing owner notification attempt. The sanitized `lead_storage_failed` log records reference, received time, failure class and whether an alert recipient is configured; it never records submitted personal data or raw SQL errors. `LEADS_ALERT_TO` must be one mailbox in the configured staff allow-list. The operational alert contains no customer details and uses an identical payload/idempotency key per UTC hour. [Resend documents a 24-hour key retention](https://resend.com/docs/dashboard/emails/idempotency-keys); this is provider deduplication of attempts, not a guarantee that an alert arrived.

Provider/configuration failure is logged as unconfirmed. Because owner notifications and operational email alerts share a provider, a separate monitored Worker-log route and assigned operator are still required before capture is enabled. No external monitor, relay or heartbeat was created. A storage reservation, a persisted row, a provider acceptance and actual email arrival remain different facts.
