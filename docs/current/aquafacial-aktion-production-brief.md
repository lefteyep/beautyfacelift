# Aquafacial Aktion Landing Page Production Brief

## 1. Reference Landing Page Analysis

Reference page: https://beautyfacelift-zuerich.ch/lp/aquafacial-microneedling/

The reference page works as a paid-traffic campaign page, not as a general website page. It uses one offer, repeated booking CTAs, a strong price anchor, long-form trust building, a mobile sticky CTA, and a Treatwell forwarding flow. The page is designed for visitors who may not know Beauty Face Lift yet and need a mix of offer clarity, local credibility, and realistic expectations before booking.

Overall strategy:

- Single campaign offer with clear before/after price comparison.
- Premium Zurich positioning through Bahnhofstrasse, Teresa Stimac, review proof, and restrained visual design.
- Long-form objection handling before the final CTA.
- Booking-first conversion flow through `/termin/`, with campaign parameters passed to Treatwell.
- Trust signals repeated above the fold, mid-page, and near booking.

Campaign angle:

- The reference page combines value, professional care, and trust.
- It avoids a cheap coupon feeling by presenting the price inside a premium layout, not as a red sale badge.
- It uses realistic cosmetic outcomes rather than absolute result claims.

Page structure:

1. Hero with headline, explanation, offer card, primary CTA, trust strip, and treatment images.
2. Problem/desire section to qualify the visitor's skin concerns.
3. Treatment explanation.
4. Benefits and realistic expectations.
5. Teresa/trust section.
6. Offer box.
7. Booking process.
8. FAQ.
9. Booking CTA block.
10. Final dark CTA.
11. Mobile sticky offer bar.

Above the fold:

- Large serif headline.
- Warm, specific subheadline.
- Price anchor and CTA shown early.
- Proof line/rating close to the CTA.
- Strong treatment imagery on the right for desktop, stacked below on mobile.

CTA strategy:

- Primary CTA appears repeatedly and routes to booking.
- CTA labels are action-specific, not generic.
- Secondary contact is lower priority and used for questions.
- Sticky mobile CTA keeps the offer available without interrupting reading.

Offer presentation:

- Price is framed as an "Aktion" and supported by treatment duration and included steps.
- The old price gives value context.
- The design stays beige/gold/premium instead of aggressive discount styling.

Trust elements:

- Teresa Stimac as named practitioner.
- 20+ years of cosmetic experience.
- 4.8/5 with 248+ reviews.
- Bahnhofstrasse 94, 8001 Zurich.
- FAQ answers that set expectations and reduce risk.

Visual hierarchy:

- Serif display headlines create premium feel.
- Compact sans body copy improves readability.
- Offer and proof modules are visually distinct but not loud.
- Cards are used for repeated items and process steps, not for every section.

Design patterns:

- Warm paper background, white cards, gold accents, dark final CTA.
- Rounded cards around 8-16px.
- Strong photographic imagery.
- Numbered process cards.
- FAQ accordion.
- Mobile sticky CTA.

What to reuse:

- Treatwell-first booking flow.
- Price/offer card pattern.
- Trust strip and Teresa block.
- FAQ accordion.
- Final dark CTA.
- Sticky mobile offer bar.
- Campaign tracking attributes and `/termin/` bridge.

What to improve for Aquafacial:

- Remove all non-Aquafacial treatment education.
- Move price and primary CTA high enough on mobile and desktop.
- Use a cleaner benefit set focused on glow, cleansing, hydration, and fresh skin.
- Make WhatsApp a clear secondary action.
- Keep the page more focused and shorter than the reference page.

What to remove or simplify:

- Any invasive-treatment explanation.
- Any aftercare copy that implies stronger downtime than Aquafacial typically needs.
- Overly complex treatment phases.
- Any competing lead form above the booking CTA.

## 2. New Aquafacial Landing Page Strategy

Main positioning:

Aquafacial Aktion in Zurich for people who want fresher, cleaner, more hydrated-looking skin through a premium, professional facial treatment at Beauty Face Lift on Bahnhofstrasse.

Main emotional angle:

"My skin looks tired or dull, and I want to feel fresh, clean, and well cared for again without a complicated treatment."

Main rational angle:

1 hour treatment, CHF 99 instead of CHF 180, includes Cleansing, gentle fruit acid peel, Hyaluron Booster, personal skin alignment, and closing care.

Primary conversion goal:

Click to `/termin/` and continue to Treatwell booking.

Secondary conversion goal:

WhatsApp question from visitors who need reassurance before booking.

Main objections:

- "Will this suit my skin?"
- "Is the CHF 99 offer credible?"
- "Is the studio professional?"
- "Will I be presentable afterwards?"
- "Is this central/easy to reach?"
- "What exactly is included?"

Trust-building strategy:

- Name Teresa Stimac early.
- Show Bahnhofstrasse address early.
- Use 20+ years and 4.8/5 with 248+ reviews.
- Explain the steps plainly.
- Use realistic wording: "kann", "wirken", "meist", "abhängig vom Hautzustand".
- Put suitability caveats into the page to increase credibility.

Offer framing strategy:

Position CHF 99 as a campaign/introductory action, not a clearance discount. The price is persuasive because it is attached to a concrete 1-hour treatment with named steps and a normal value of CHF 180.

Paid ads connection:

- Hero headline mirrors ad promise: Aquafacial Aktion in Zurich.
- Price and duration visible before scroll.
- UTM and creative angle parameters remain preserved through `/termin/`.
- Retargeting users see the same offer, trust proof, and booking path.

Cold traffic strategy:

- Do not assume brand familiarity.
- Explain what Aquafacial is within the first few sections.
- Show local trust before asking for commitment again.
- Keep CTA labels concrete and low-friction.

## 6. Design & UI/UX Requirements

Visual style:

- Premium, calm, cosmetic, local Zurich.
- Warm paper background, white cards, gold accents, dark final CTA.
- Avoid red sale badges, countdowns, popups, and aggressive scarcity.

Color direction:

- Paper: warm off-white.
- Cards: white/bone.
- Text: dark espresso/charcoal.
- Accent: muted gold.
- Final CTA: dark charcoal with light text.

Typography:

- Serif display headlines for premium feel.
- Sans body text for readability.
- No negative letter spacing.
- No viewport-width font scaling that risks overflow.

Buttons:

- 8px radius.
- Gold primary button.
- Outline secondary button for WhatsApp.
- Mobile buttons full-width where needed.
- CTA text must remain concise and action-specific.

Cards:

- Use cards for repeated items: benefits, process, proof.
- Do not nest cards inside cards.
- Keep card radius restrained.

Images:

- Use real treatment/studio/Teresa imagery.
- Hero should show actual treatment context, not abstract beauty stock.
- Secondary image can show calm treatment room.

Icons:

- Use small benefit icons only where they improve scanning.
- Keep icons subtle and gold-toned.

Offer box:

- White or warm card.
- Price prominent but premium.
- Old price as context, not shame.
- Include duration and treatment steps.

CTA banner:

- Mobile sticky offer bar with CHF 99 and "Termin sichern".
- Must not overlap cookie banner or final CTA content.

FAQ:

- Accordion with clear Aquafacial-only questions.
- Answers should be short, realistic, and trust-building.

Mobile rules:

- First screen: headline, price, one primary CTA, trust signal.
- One-column cards.
- Enough bottom padding for sticky CTA.
- No horizontal overflow.
